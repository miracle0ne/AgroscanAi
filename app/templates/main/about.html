WHAT IS AGROSCAN AI?

Understand Your Plant Better

AgroScan AI is an AI-powered plant health analysis platform that helps farmers get preliminary insights about possible plant diseases from a plant image.

Select a supported crop, upload a clear plant image, and receive information about the possible disease, severity, symptoms and recommended actions.
